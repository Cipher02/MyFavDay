02 · A first I’ll always remember

♡

My first kiss.
With you.

There, in the car, we shared a kiss.

There are moments you know you’ll remember while they’re still happening.

That was one of mine.

My first kiss wasn’t something I had to keep imagining anymore. It was real, and it was with you.

I’m so happy it was you, Ma.

Of all the firsts still ahead of me, I’ll always be grateful for this one.

04 · Freezing weather, very warm hugs

It was freezing. I still wouldn’t have been anywhere else.

We were cold, the floor was slippery, and Shree and I apparently decided that standing upright was optional.

But then there were your hugs.

The photographs. The kisses. The way we kept looking out for each other.

I loved all the little moments of making sure you were okay—and feeling you do the same for me.

07 · The part where I wanted another minute

Just one more minute.

Opening the gifts with you. Being close to you. Sharing a few more kisses before it was time to go.

By then, I wasn’t thinking about what we had planned or where we had been.

I just wanted a little more time beside you.

And now we’re back on video calls.

Your face on my screen again.

Except now, when I say I miss your hug, there’s a particular hug I’m remembering.

♡One more thing, Ma…

Ma,

I keep thinking about you coming to get me.

We had joked about keeping a metre between us, and then there we were, running towards each other. I think I’ll smile about that for a very long time.

Thank you for this day.

For picking me up. For bringing me into a little more of your world. For the laughter, the kisses, and the way you cared for me in all those small moments.

I’m so happy I got to meet Roshna and Shree. I’m also going to remain just a little bit proud of passing that interview.

But the moments I keep coming back to are the quiet ones. Holding you close in the cold. Being beside you in the car. Looking at you and knowing I didn’t have to say ‘I wish you were here’—because you were.

And my first kiss will always be a memory of you. That means so much to me.

I wanted to make you happy with those little gifts. You made me happy just by being there, being yourself, and sharing the day with me.

We’re back to our calls now, and I already miss you.

But I’m glad we have this. A day we can talk about, laugh about, and return to whenever the distance feels a little too long.

Until I get to hug you again, I hope this page feels like a little piece of that hug.

I love you, Ma.

— Asvineee
